System Architecture

How ROADGUARD AI works in the real world

Helmet camera

A rider mounts a camera on the helmet. It captures road frames continuously, the AI detects potholes, GPS records the location, a complaint is generated and the rider gets status updates.

Vehicle camera

The forward-facing camera already in the vehicle is analyzed while driving. Each detection flows: AI detection → GPS → complaint → authority → repair → notification.
